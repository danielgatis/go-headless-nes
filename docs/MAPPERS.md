# Mappers

One table, straight from the mapper factory: every mapper ID the loader
recognizes, the board it maps to, and whether it is implemented. That is
287 supported IDs, covering effectively the whole licensed NES/Famicom
library plus a large slice of the unlicensed, multicart and pirate
space. Both iNES 1 and NES 2.0 headers work, including 12-bit mapper
IDs and submappers. Expansion audio is mixed into the APU output for
VRC6, Sunsoft 5B, MMC5, Namco 163 and the DripGame sample
channels.
